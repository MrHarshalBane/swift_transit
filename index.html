System Architecture • Business Rules Compliance Matrix

Verifiable compliance with all specifications of the business model:

Rule 3.5.1

Username & Password Security

Username strictly 3-15 chars (alphanumeric & _). Passwords stored exclusively as cryptographically secure SHA-256 hashes via Web Crypto API.

Rule 3.5.2

Date Validation (dd-MM-yyyy)

All travel dates are validated against the standard format and past dates are rejected.

Rule 3.5.3 & 3.5.4

Seat Constraints & Fare Formula

Seats booked per travel date; double-booking prevented; maximum 6 seats per booking. Total Fare = Fare × Seats.

Rule 3.5.5

Cancellation & 90% Refund

90% of fare refunded, 10% cancellation charge deducted. Released seats instantly return to availability.

Rule 3.5.6

Active Booking Deletion Guard

Buses with active bookings cannot be removed by an administrator, preventing orphaned reservations.

Rule 3.5.7

First-Run Auto-Seed

Default administrator (admin / admin123) and 4 sample buses created on initialization.
